Sound familiar?

Small-space growing goes wrong in the same three ways.

None of them are your fault. They come from advice written for big gardens. Each one has a chapter that fixes it.

“My tomatoes grew tall, then did nothing.”

Big varieties in small pots put everything into leaves. Picking the right variety matters more than any fertilizer.

FixChapter 7, Varieties Bred for Pots

“I only get sun in the afternoon.”

Four hours of light is enough for more than you’d think. You just have to match each plant to the sun you actually get.

FixChapter 3, Mapping Your Light

“Everything dies in the heat wave.”

Pots dry out up to three times faster than garden beds. A simple watering routine and the right pot size keep plants alive.

FixChapter 9, Water Without Worry

What you’ll be able to do

By the end of one season, you’ll know your balcony like a farmer knows a field.

Read your light

Measure how many hours of sun each corner gets, then use that to choose what goes where.

Pick the right pots

Know the minimum pot size for each crop in litres and gallons, so roots never run out of room.

Plant on schedule

Follow a week-by-week calendar that tells you when to sow, move and pick in your climate zone.

Water less, lose less

Use self-watering setups and mulch so a long weekend away doesn’t cost you the crop.

Feed your soil

Mix your own potting soil and feed it with kitchen scraps, so you buy fewer bags each spring.

Harvest all year

Plan succession sowings and cold-hardy greens so there’s something to pick in every month.

Is it for you?

Written for renters, beginners and anyone short on space.

You’ll love it if you…

  • have a balcony, patio, rooftop or a sunny windowsill
  • have never grown food before, or tried and gave up
  • rent, and can’t dig, drill or build anything permanent
  • want a plan for the whole year, not just spring

It’s probably not for you if you…

  • already run a large in-ground vegetable garden
  • want an indoor hydroponics or grow-light setup
  • need a printed hardcover (it’s digital only, for now)

Meet the author

Hi, I’m R. Linden.

I started growing on a third-floor balcony with two cracked pots and a packet of basil seeds. Eight years and five apartments later, that same kind of space feeds me salad from April to November and more tomatoes than my neighbours know what to do with.

I wrote this book because every guide I read assumed I had a backyard. Everything in it has been tested in real pots, on real balconies, by someone who has killed a lot of plants along the way.

“You don’t need more space. You need the right plants in the right pots, at the right time.”
