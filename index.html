Is it for you?

Written for anyone shopping and cooking for one.

You’ll get the most from it if you…

  • live alone, or cook for one or two
  • watch every dollar, or live on a fixed income
  • throw away more food than you’d like
  • want a plan for storms, power cuts and bad days

It’s probably not for you if you…

  • cook for a large family every night
  • want a list of this week’s store prices (they change too fast, so the book teaches the method instead)
  • need a printed paperback (it’s a PDF, for now)

Questions

Before you buy

Something else on your mind? Leave a comment on any of Bev’s videos.

Is this a printed book?

No. It’s a PDF you download right after you buy. You can read it on a computer, tablet or phone, and print any pages you like, including all eight printables.

Is the print easy to read?

Yes. The text is set large on full letter-size pages, with clear headings, short paragraphs and lots of space. On a tablet or phone you can zoom in as far as you like.

Does it list store prices?

No, on purpose. Prices change every week. The book teaches you how to read a shelf tag, find your own “buy price” and work out the cost per use, which works at any price in any store.

Is the safety advice reliable?

The food-safety, power-outage and emergency advice is checked against the USDA, FoodSafety.gov, FDA, CDC, American Red Cross and National Fire Protection Association, and every source is listed in the back. It’s general information, not medical advice. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about your own health.

Who is Bev?

Bev is the host of the Cart for One with Bev channel: 72, in a small house in Indiana, with an old terrier named Pepper and a spiral notebook of prices. The book is written in her voice, just like the videos, and the tips and safety facts in it are real and sourced.

I don’t live in the US. Will it help?

The store names and agencies are American, but the methods work anywhere: unit prices, portioning and freezing, the fridge and freezer rules, and the check-in habit.
